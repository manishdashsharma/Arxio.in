## What Arxio Does

### Upload any PDF

Drop in a research paper, book, or report. Arxio reads it completely and builds a full workspace — ready in minutes, not hours.

Every workspace includes:

- **Paper Overview** — what the paper says in plain English
- **Concept Explainer** — every technical term explained simply, with analogies
- **Methodology Breakdown** — what the researchers actually did, step by step
- **Results Analysis** — what the numbers mean and how they compare to prior work
- **Critical Analysis** — real strengths, real weaknesses, open questions
- **15-Slide Presentation** — beautiful deck with real paper content, ready to present
- **Presentation Script** — word-for-word what to say, slide by slide
- **Q&A Preparation** — 10 questions your professor will likely ask, with perfect answers
- **Cheat Sheet** — one-page reference for key terms, results, and talking points
- **Full Bundle** — everything above in a single download

You should never need to open the original paper again.

### Research any topic

Type any topic. Arxio searches the web and academic databases, reads everything it finds, and generates a professional document with real citations — in the format you need.

- PowerPoint presentation
- Word document
- PDF report
- Excel with data and charts
- Academic research report
